# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-18)

First release.

- `SKILL.md` router: scope, task routing, intake, eight principles, quick rules with their target-SDK gates, a review checklist and a "verify it" section.
- 20 references: the method (`applying.md`), philosophy and psychology kept apart from Google's own statements, foundations, Material 3 Expressive and the expression dial, adaptive layouts with the enforcement matrix, components, patterns (navigation, permissions, sign-in, notifications, Live Updates, states, writing), accessibility as acceptance criteria, quality checklists and Android vitals, an architecture contract for designers, iOS-to-Android translation as decisions, one file each for Wear OS, TV, cars, XR and AI glasses, and widgets, a version matrix and a glossary.
- A claim ledger (`ledger/claims.tsv`, not shipped) that ties every load-bearing number to a Google source; conflicts between Google's pages are stated as conflicts.
- A `.living/` sidecar for facts that change between releases.
- `npx android-design` installer.
